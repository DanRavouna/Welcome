## The player's road (defined Nov 2026, with Dan; the structure every change hangs on)
Order follows the gates (zones at trainer level 1/3/6/9, climbs at 11/13, the Watch at four seals). Each station: what you do there, what it means, where that metaphor was first met, and its status by the rule: TOLD (text carries it), HINT ONLY (seen, nothing to do), PLAY, NO INTRO (done, never introduced), INTRODUCED + PLAYED. Music is not audited yet (listed as a gap, not a pass).
1. **Opening** (title, Fern, "Choose your partner"). Meaning: the whole experience introduced; you begin kept, given a partner you did not earn. Built (Nov 2026): the title screen is one lit window in the dark and the game begins when you touch it (`#title`, `showTitle`); Fern says two lines. Status: INTRODUCED (the first hint of the whole game); the partner is still received, which is the point.
2. **Sproutvale** (Mom, the Lab, Pip, Mira, the two cottages, the well). Meaning: where you are kept; the window is lit for you. Introduced by: the title screen. Rebuilt (Nov 2026, Dan: players lost the direction in the first minute): you begin at the door of Home with the window lit above you (kept for you while you have one critter, and again at three seals), tall grass 2 tiles from the door (the first keep is the first thing played), the one open gate west 15 tiles down the road with a lamp lit on it by day (`gate-lamp`, `day: true`), the three others shut behind barricades; Fern names the gate with the lamp and the grass by your door. The two chairs at Home introduce "a seat counted for you" before the arch. `openingtest` (real defaults) measures the tiles to the first grass and to the gate. Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED (the first keep), and the kept window is played again at the end, station 13.
3. **Moss Forest** (level 1). Meaning: being tended while you grow. Play: rare grass stirs for the one who stays. Introduced by: the sprout rows (z-sprouts). Status: PLAY, weakly introduced (sprouts are a hint of growth, not of staying).
4. **Mossbrook, Wren's trial**. Meaning: tending is staying beside it; the first fire. Play: the bed grows only while you stand by it. Introduced by: the Moss stir (same verb, stay). Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED.
5. **Highland Pass and the Sealed Arch** (level 3). Meaning: the road kept by people you never meet; the seat, who is counted. Play: the lantern road lights ahead of you (seen, not done); the clues are things you look at; the hall is a fight and the ninth seat answers. Status: HINT ONLY for the road; the arch is PLAY, NO INTRO (counting a seat is never met before).
6. **Frost Peaks, Frosthold, Glacia's trial** (level 3). Meaning: what is kept by being kept cold; waiting. Play: ice forms only while you stand still. Introduced by: the zone itself, no wind, snow falling straight (ambiance as the hint). Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED. Elements agree (wind 0, straight snow, the still pool).
7. **Ember Fields, Cinderport, Blaze's trial** (level 6). Meaning: the fire that has to be fed; work. Play: carry logs, keep three fires alive at once. Introduced by: the hearth with fresh logs in the fields, the chimney smoke in town. Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED.
8. **Tide Shore, Coralbay, Coral's trial** (level 9). Meaning: what the sea gives back; return; the road home begins. Play: in the dark, answer the lamps' call in order. Introduced by: the returned things on Tide Shore itself now (z-returned by the water, Nov 2026), then the row and the chair on Glacier Coast. Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED.
9. **The routes** (Ashwood, Glacier Coast, Reef Causeway). Meaning: the roads between the fires, kept walkable by people you mostly never meet; each holds a thing to look at (hearthstone, brass plate, wreck stone). Play: looking; after the beacon, keeping them yourself (station 13). Status: HINT until the beacon, PLAYED after it.
10. **The Cold Ascent and the Lamp Tower** (level 11, from Frosthold). Meaning: the light, the way home, lit by you. Play (rebuilt Nov 2026): the tower is dark; each chamber has a lamp on a stand that takes while you stay beside it (2.4 s, fades if you leave), and its gate opens; nothing closes behind you; the great lamp lights only from the three you kept. Introduced by: Wren's bed (staying), the climb's posts you light by passing (`own`), the window. Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED.
11. **The Cinder Descent and the Forge Below** (level 13, from Cinderport). Meaning: the fire; the work; where it began. Play: carry two cores to the hub sockets, Hale, relight the furnace. Introduced by: Blaze's logs (same verb, carry and feed). Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED, the weaker twin of Blaze.
12. **Vigil Peak and the Long Watch** (four seals). Meaning: the long watch; a night is kept by lasting it. Play (rebuilt Nov 2026): the night runs in the lobby itself (`duelHall`), the visitor comes in at the door, the night has a length (28 s + 1.6 s per night, +10 s on the long nights; `window.__nightS` overrides) and ends at dawn with the party still standing, kept, or earlier if they all go down; the duel box shows the night running out, not a body count; HP carries over, two watches a day. The turn-based fallback (no follower) is unchanged. Introduced by: every lamp kept before it; the lobby's lamps that do not go out. Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED.
13. **The ending**: the lamp lit, the ninth seat taken, the keepers rest. Meaning: you no longer need someone to keep it for you. Play (built Nov 2026): from the beacon on, the window at Home is dark until you light it, each day (`homeLit` = today's date), and no road post lights itself ahead of you any more: a post lights when you pass and stays lit, saved (`own:` on every trail). Mom only says the window was dark when she came down. Status: INTRODUCED + PLAYED, daily.
14. **The daily loop** (20 rolls, 40 sightings, keep or release, quests, titles, the Dex; the Arena ladder off the Ashwood Trail from level 6, one rival a day). Meaning: a day holds what it holds and nothing carries over; what you keep comes home with you. Built (Nov 2026): the rolls chip is a lamp whose flame goes down as the day is spent (`#c-oil`); the spent-day line says the day's light is spent; the critter you keep sleeps in the Lab basket (`critterbasket` draws the follower's sprite). Status: INTRODUCED (the lamp) + PLAYED (Keep is the loop's verb); release and the Shrine unchanged.
