## The sentence (the greater meaning)
"Home is something someone keeps lit for you, and the journey ends when you become the one who keeps it." Dan's refinement: "you become the one who keeps it means that you don't need someone to keep it for you anymore." The ending is independence, not inheritance. Guard rails: being looked after is good, not weakness; carrying your own light is not being alone (home and its people stay intact at the end); withdrawn support must be felt, not look like a bug.

## The last act (as built)

The process now says: the last act must be done UNASKED (nothing tells the player to do it; the road has made them want to). Judge whether it is.

## The rules (from CLAUDE.md and docs/process.md)
- Hint and play the metaphor, don't tell it. PLAY: the meaning is something the player does; the mechanic itself carries it, not a text or a prop that rhymes with it. EVERY ELEMENT TOGETHER: mechanic, layout, what is seen and hidden, colours, sounds and music, words and pacing must convey the same intent; an element that says something else, or nothing, is a bug of meaning. HINT FIRST: every metaphor is introduced before it is played, smaller and quieter; a hint with nothing to play afterwards is the weak form. DON'T TELL: no text explains the meaning or recites a rule a sign already states.
- The road is worked backwards from the last act. The unit is the STATION: meaning (what it says inside the sentence), hint (the earlier, quieter encounter), act (what the player does that carries it). A few paths may lead to the same act. Hidden things are stations too.
- Dan's principles, verbatim: "trust our players to be intelligent persons"; "sense of mystery is based on hinting but never telling so that the player can project himself"; "the winds symbolize change ... in the cold ... stillness"; "the gyms should be a trial, with a puzzle themed to ... the meaning of the type"; "a game is a whole and playing it means experimenting all of its elements at once, and they must convey a similar intent"; "most players loose interest after a minute of playing, the initial direction is difficult to feel"; "remove all of the words above the buildings and replace them with symbolic marks"; "Creation starts where expectation ends".
