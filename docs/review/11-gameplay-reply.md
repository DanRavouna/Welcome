## 2. Where we disagree, by item, with the counter-proposal

**CD-7 Gates by fires: "Do not touch the leaders' team levels (they carry the balance)."** Disagree on exactly that clause. The level gates were doing pacing work: a player reached Blaze at Lv 6+ because a number said so. Remove the gate and the leader's team is the only wall, met by a lower party. Tonight Blaze (Lv 15, stage 3) is 20% mash / 0% verbs against stage 2 Lv 12, Coral 3% in the handoff's run. Gating by fires with those levels makes Blaze a wall of days with nothing else open. Counter: Blaze `lv: 11`, Coral `lv: 12` as first guesses, each simmed with the party a player has when the previous fire is kept (stage 2 Lv 9 to 11 at Blaze; stage 2 to 3 Lv 12 at Coral), the target being the handoff's own expected win rates (46 and 33) or better. The levels are DAN, the sim is the proof, and the item is not signable without it. 08's "town north gate at Glacia's seal so the Pass is walked first": accepted, it answers 07-F19.

**CD-12 The day's lamp: "Do not touch `KEEP_COST`, 20 rolls, until a human day is measured."** Disagree on the hold. The fuse is a proportion, not a speed: 20 rolls at an average of about 2 per keep is ten keeps whatever the pace, and ten keeps is the first quarter of a 20-minute session for anyone (Dan's own 2.5 hours over three days is about 50 minutes a day; the rolls were gone in the first fifteen). The sightings term is dead on top of that: 30 of 40 left after a full bot day, so the "+1 when sightings are gone" never fires and the counter only confuses. Counter: `KEEP_COST` Common 1, Uncommon 1, Rare 2, Mythic 4; drop `FREE_SIGHTINGS` from `actKeepCost`; 20 rolls stay (the flame must still go down in one session). And after the rolls the first 15 kills a day pay 1 trainer XP each (the essence cap exists; the second half of a session should move something). Unresolved for Dan if 08 holds; the rest of CD-12 (chip, ticket, woken Rare, dead quests, the toast) is signed.

**CD-13 Help that works, then leaves: "an item slot, or the five items out."** Disagree with both options. A fifth wheel slot is a fifth target under a thumb; removing the items means the help never arrives, so its leaving is nothing. Counter: no button: in a duel, when the lead drops under 30% HP and the bag holds a Potion (Super first) it is used for you, a number and no toast; Remedy clears burn/sap/weak the same way; Ward and Whetstone apply at duel start if held (`Game.hit` already reads `hero.ward`, `hero.boost`). That is being looked after, played. Price 0 from two fires; the stall gone at four and three Potions on Home's shelf each morning. 08 already marks the mechanism DAN; I ask that auto-use be the first option he sees.

**CD-14 Letting go is return: `releaseGain` 0 now.** Disagree on timing only. Release is a real income after the 15-kill essence cap (Common 1, Rare 2, times stage), and evolve (15 / 60) is the sink; cutting the income alone slows the one visible growth. Counter: ship the `returned` pool, the quest removal and the label now (no economy effect); hold `releaseGain = 0` until `milesim.py` is re-run with whatever `KEEP_COST` Dan picks, and ship it with M9 or with a lower evolve price, as one change. 08 marks it DAN (economy); this is the line for him.

**CD-15 The verbs: `st.vcd` now, the `open` window "later, with a moving bot."** Disagree on deferring `open`. Nobody plans a moving bot. The slowness (verb fights 2 to 3x longer in every row tonight) is bot-independent: a verb is control, and control earns nothing because a held enemy is hurt only by the quick slash. `m.open = 1.5` s on `frozen`/`rooted`/`pull`/`daze`/`calmT` with `strike()` at 1.5x is one line and the existing sim measures it. Counter: both now, the proof two lines from `duelsim.py 2 12 40 wren glacia bram kiki` (smart within 10 points of mash, smart fight time within 1.3x). Unresolved if 08 holds.
