# 01. The road and the meaning (review of `src/world-rev/`, Nov 2026)

## For the directors

1. The last act is not done unasked. It is asked for seven times before the player reaches it: the Titles list ("Lamplighter: Relight the old beacon") from day one, the Frosthold sign, the world-map card ("A climb to an old beacon"), the Journal, Old Marta, the Dex row "A spark is said to wait inside the old beacon", and Warden Maren's "The lamp is yours to wake. Touch the glass."
2. What makes the player want it today is loot: +30 essence, a jackpot jingle, a secret critter, a title. Nothing on the road makes them want light.
3. The road never puts the player in the dark without a keeper. Home's window is lit from the first second to the end (`homeLitNow`, `objState`); the lantern road lights itself ahead on two maps only, and only between 18:00 and 05:00 on the real clock. Most players never meet the thing the ending takes away.
4. The withdrawal of help is words only, by the code's own comment ("Words only; no price, reward or rule changes"). Mira says "Take what you need" and charges the same price. After the beacon Mom says the window "stays on" and "was dark when I came down" in the same conversation (verified in the build).
5. The ending says the opposite of the sentence in two places: after the lamp every lighthouse beams by itself "day and night" and the toast says "Its light reaches the whole land" (the land is kept for you, more than before), and the great lamp is gated by a boss fight, not by the three lamps.
6. The "last act" is in the middle of the game: the Ascent opens at trainer level 11, before the fourth seal for most players; the Long Watch (twenty nights, daily), the Forge (13) and the Arena come after it, and the Forge ends with the same +30, critter and title.
7. Of the fourteen stations, six are played with a real mechanic (Wren, Glacia, Blaze, Coral, the three tower lamps, the Watch's night), three are looked at (the routes, the arch's clues, the returned things), and the rest are told. Nine of the fourteen have a line that states their meaning out loud.
8. There are no hidden stations. All four secret critters are on-path rewards announced as "???" rows with hints in the Dex. The ninth seat has a meaning and a hint but its act is a fight and a tap.
9. The daily act of lighting the window has no consequence anywhere in the code except a yellow rectangle on the town map, needs a visit inside Home, and the button says "Examine".
10. What the road is missing for the act to be wanted: one night of darkness before the end (a window nobody lit), one keeper who visibly stops, one time the player's own light mattered to someone else, and silence around the act (no title, no toast, no hint).

## 1. What this element means now

Read as built, the game says: "A land of keepers holds dark things until a strong enough trainer beats them and collects what they kept." The sentence the record wants ("home is something someone keeps lit for you, and the journey ends when you become the one who keeps it; you no longer need it kept for you") exists in the words (the keepers' speeches, Mom's lines, Marta, the tablet) and in four trial mechanics that genuinely play tending (stay, be still, feed, answer), but the spine of the road is still conquest: a level gate, a sign naming the prize, three trainers, a boss, a touch, a reward, a title. The ending's mechanics that do carry the sentence (the window dark until you light it, the posts lit by passing) are real but reach almost nobody: they need a visit inside Home, a road that only two maps have, and a night the real clock gives only to evening players. The player arrives at the great lamp wanting essence and a critter, told to touch the glass, having never once missed a light.
