# 03. The places and their layout (review of `src/world-rev/`, Nov 2026)

## For the directors
1. The first screen is the strongest layout in the game: one lit window in the dark, then the door of that house with the window lit above you and grass two steps away. It is let down by two things: the first voice (Fern) speaks from thirty tiles off screen, and the largest thing in the first view is a pond that says nothing.
2. The sentence's own object fails at night: after the great lamp, Home's windows are lit by the generic night glow even when the player has not lit them (`homeLitNow()` false, both windows yellow). The one daily act of being the keeper is undone by a draw loop.
3. The hints are placed where the player will not walk. `addSpot` puts each zone's and city's signature thing on the "least open" tile, which is a corner: Frost's flame in ice, Ember's hearth, Tide's returned things, Frosthold's kettle, Mossbrook's growth post are all 25 to 31 tiles from the gate and off the road to the city. Only Moss's sprouts and Cinderport's workbench are met on the way.
4. The land contradicts itself in three places: Coralbay's sea is on the far side from the shore you arrive along; the Glacier Coast's "row beside the water" lies in a snowfield and exists only from 05:00 to 06:59 real time; the Cinder Descent is the Cold Ascent's grid reversed (same seed), so the cold's stillness and the fire's work share one shape.
5. Generated sameness, honestly: the four cities are one plan (gym at the same lot, shop and three houses at the same lots), the twenty route boxes are one recipe with a different line on a cairn, the twenty rooms are one room, and the two climbs are one map. Palette and words carry the differences; layout carries almost none.
6. Three texts in the dungeons tell the metaphor or a stale rule: the Lamp Tower's tablet ("The way up is lit by the one who climbs it"), its gates ("Somewhere a lever works it": there are no levers), the Forge's gate ("Two sockets in the hub must wake first") and Vigil Peak's sign ("Twenty nights, one lamp").
7. The seat is played at level 3 (the arch) and hinted at level 9 (the chairs on the Skerry and the Coast); the only earlier hint, the two chairs at Home, is behind a door the opening leads away from. A ring of eight stones with a gap on the Highland Gate would fix the order with one prop move.
8. What works and should be kept: the Sealed Arch hall (the fire between you and the ninth seat, the rubble behind the seat), the four gym halls (the whole trial in one view, the leader behind the gate), Frost Peaks' still pool at the entrance, Cinderport's workbench on the entry road, the Ascent's ledges seen through the cliff rows, the Watch lit at night.
9. The dawn and dusk gates on the route things (`WILD_RULES`) hide five hints from anyone who plays by day; the shore row is the worst case because its chair stays and faces nothing.
10. Thirty-four findings below, eleven of them bugs of meaning; most are a list of cells to move, not a rebuild. Feel, tempo, phone reading of the small props and the night tint on a real screen could not be judged from here.

## 1. What the places mean now
As built, the world says "a hub with four zones, each with a town, each town with a gym", and the sentence is laid over it in objects and lines. The opening says it well (the window, the door, the grass, the lit gate). The Sealed Arch hall says it in layout (the fire between you and the empty seat). The gym halls say their trial in layout. Everywhere else the layout is a generator's recipe, and the meaning lives in a prop placed by a "least open tile" rule that hides it, in a sign, or in a line said by someone standing in a corner. The three contradictions (Coralbay's sea on the wrong side, a coast with no coast, a descent that is the ascent upside down) are the generator talking louder than the sentence. The daily act at Home is undone at night by the night-window glow. The draft meanings in the handoff (kept; tended while you grow; kept cold; fed; given back; the seat, the light, the fire; the long watch) are right, and in two places the player already meets them before the act (the still pool before Glacia, the sprouts before Wren); in the rest the hint is present on the map but not on the player's way.

## Per place: what the layout says now, what it should say
- **Title** (`shell.html` `#title`, `showTitle`): one lit window, a red roof (Home's), the game begins at the touch. Says: someone kept this lit before you did anything. Keep.
- **Sproutvale** (`buildTown`, 44x34): you start at Home's door, the left window lit by day (`homeLitNow`), grass at (5-6, 9-11) two tiles away, the west gate lamp at (3,15) lit by day, three other gates barricaded. Says "kept" well. Weak points: the pond (7-15 x 9-14) fills the right third of the first view and says nothing; the gate lamp sits on the last visible column, under the "To Moss Forest" tag; Fern is at (37,11), off screen for her lines; the well ("the bucket is up, and full. Someone drew it before you came") is 41 tiles away at (33,25); the Vigil Peak trail is a second locked north road beside the Frost gate.
- **Moss Forest** (`buildZone('moss')`): the sprouts at (4,17) stand beside the west exit to Mossbrook: hint on the way. The rest is the zone recipe (ring road, six blobs, pool, rare corner).
- **Mossbrook** (`buildMossbrook`): grove houses, the gym north-centre, the pond south, the growth post at (9,16) off the gate-to-gym line (d=25). The trees come up to the border only; "built around the old trees" is said by the house art, not by the plan.
- **Highland Pass network** (five boxes): the ruin at (9,14) west of the entry road, out of the arrival view; two lamp posts on the Pass; Windy Lookout is flat; Hermit's Cliff is a good nook (cups, chair-less, a lamp that is always lit at night).
- **Frost Peaks**: the still pool at (24,20) is in the arrival view; the flame in ice at (8,4) is in the far corner.
- **Frosthold**: the avenue climbs between two terraces to the gym: the one city whose plan says its meaning (a climb into the cold, a palisade). The kettle at (31,8) sits on the Cold Ascent road, met at level 11.
- **Ember Fields**: the lava pool beside the entry road is seen at once; the hearth with fresh logs at (6,23) is in the far corner.
- **Cinderport**: the entry road passes Tamsin, the Smithy and the workbench (29,11): the one city whose signature is on the way. The forge arch (ember chimneys, orange windows) does the rest.
- **Tide Shore**: the ocean strip is x 27-33; both things (moored skiff (26,7), returned things (26,23)) are by the water but 8 tiles off the boardwalk; the returned "row" is one tile drawn as a comb.
- **Coralbay**: sea on the east, two piers, the boardwalk, the harbour lamp; the arrival from the west shows palms and the shop, no water.
- **Ashwood / Glacier Coast / Reef Causeway networks**: one recipe; the Reef's causeway strips are the only shape that means something (the lamps' light over water). The Coast has 33 water cells.
- **The Arena**: the ring in the north with stands reads as a ring; the camp (five tents, a fire) is 30 tiles from the door.
- **Cold Ascent**: five ledges joined by ramps, the next ledge seen through the one-tile cliff row, posts that light as you pass; the beacon's lantern is not in view from the last ledge (view top y≈7, lantern y 3-4).
- **Cinder Descent**: the Ascent's grid reversed with the same seed; the forge mouth in a straight south wall.
- **Sealed Arch** (`sealedArch`): a round hall, the fire at its centre, eight statues in a ring, the ninth seat straight across the fire from the door, the rubble to the north right behind the seat: the best dungeon layout in the game. The seat is at the edge of the fire's light and reads as a dark lump.
- **Lamp Tower** (`lampTower`): four identical chambers up a flagstone line, the lamp in alternating corners, torches "by the door" placed at the top of the first chamber beside the gate, a tablet that tells.
- **Forge Below** (`forgeBelow`): a round hub, two mirror halls, the furnace north: bright (dark r 5.2) which fits the fire; the halls differ only by the core's colour.
- **Vigil Peak and the Long Watch**: a 12-tile straight road to a lit castle seen whole on arrival; a 12x9 lobby. Nothing long.
- **The interiors** (20 rooms): one 12x9 room, objects on the back wall and the corners, the resident at (7,3), the whole room in view. Home: the two chairs are the first thing in reach (d=1): good; the window lamp sits on the north wall while the lit window faces the road to the south.
